# PocketWise — AI collaboration record

AI tools were used as support during the development of this academic design project. They assisted with brainstorming the student budgeting concept, organising the Design Book and presentation, drafting initial UX documentation, proposing user flows and wireframe structures, generating and refining front-end code, preparing a Google Apps Script/Sheets integration example, and checking the submission against the course objectives.

The student selected the PocketWise concept, defined the target audience and project scope, reviewed and adapted the generated material, remains responsible for understanding and explaining the design and code, and must run the prototype and verify all technical claims before submission.

AI-generated personas, user needs, friction points and success criteria are design hypotheses, not primary research findings. No interviews or usability tests are claimed as completed unless the student conducts them and records the results. The Google Sheets connector is a prepared example and must not be described as live until deployed and tested. The student should follow the institution's academic integrity policy and retain prompts/revision history if required.
